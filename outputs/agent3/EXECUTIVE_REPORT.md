## CEO view

1. **Growth is strong and profit is growing faster than sales.** Net sales rose 46.2% while modeled operating profit rose 63.0%, because fixed operating expense grew only 13.7%. Modeled operating margin moved from 27.8% to 31.0%.
2. **Growth came from more customers, not bigger baskets.** Orders +42.4% and purchasing members +63.5%; AOV only +2.7%. Repeat purchaser share fell 4.1 points to 73.4%, so the newer customer base is buying less often within the period.
3. **Still slightly short of plan year to date.** Attainment 98.0%, 20,393.52 below target, with 7 of 10 months under 100%. October 2026 broke the pattern at 106.3%.

## CFO view

1. **Margin is above target in every category.** Gross margin 46.0% against a flat 42% target. Home and Kitchen leads at 48.3%; Apparel is lowest at 44.4%.
2. **Four high-volume Electronics SKUs carry low margins.** Over the full period SKU-0001 (28.2%), SKU-0004 (28.7%), SKU-0007 (34.0%) and SKU-0009 (34.8%) generated 287,456.04 of net sales, 15.1% of the total, all at least 10 points under the company average of 45.4%.
3. **Marketing efficiency is flat while spend scales with sales.** Spend rose 45.7%, in line with net sales, holding efficiency at about 11.9 and spend at 8.4% of net sales. Net cash exceeds net sales because captures include tax and shipping; the bridge reconciles to the cent (Agent 2 report).

## Sales Executive view

1. **Website is the main source of the target gap.** Website attainment is 90.3% (growth +38.5%) versus Mobile App 108.2% (growth +55.8%) and Marketplace 97.7%. The five largest misses are all Website or Marketplace cells: US Website -18,061.54, CA Website -15,988.34 (73.0% attainment), GB Website -6,138.39, US Marketplace -5,676.14, DE Website -4,971.98.
2. **Canada is the weakest market.** Lowest attainment (85.8%), slowest growth (+28.8%) and lowest marketing efficiency (10.39). CA Website efficiency is 8.67, the lowest of any market and channel. Over-target markets: India 108.8%, Germany 102.4%.
3. **Apparel returns are about double or triple other categories.** Apparel unit return rate is 12.6% in Jan to Oct 2026 versus 3.7% to 7.6% elsewhere. The six highest-return SKUs are all Apparel (13.3% to 16.2%). Apparel generated 32,700.33 of all-time merchandise refunds, 24.7% of the total, on 11.5% of net sales.

## Operations

40.6% of delivered shipments arrived after the promised date (6,476 delivered, average 5.0 days from order to delivery). The rate is between 37% and 43% for every carrier and every month, so no single carrier explains it. Jan to Oct 2026 late rate: Carrier A 37.0%, Carrier C 41.0%, Carrier B 42.1%.

## Recommended actions (recommendations; causes are hypotheses until tested)

1. **Diagnose Website conversion in the US and Canada.** Website converts 17.8 orders per 1,000 sessions versus 19.7 for Mobile App. Hypothesis: app-specific experience or pricing drives the difference. Test by comparing funnel steps, which this dataset does not contain.
2. **Review Canada budget and Website spend.** CA Website returns 8.67 of net sales per marketing dollar versus 13.53 average for Mobile App. Consider shifting incremental budget toward Mobile App and Germany or India, where efficiency is above 13.
3. **Reprice or renegotiate cost on SKU-0001, SKU-0004, SKU-0007 and SKU-0009.** A move to the 42% target margin on these SKUs is a CFO decision; quantify with supplier cost data not in this dataset.
4. **Target Apparel returns.** Size or fit accounts for 181 of 558 returned Apparel units. Hypothesis: better size guidance reduces returns. Track the Apparel unit return rate monthly.
5. **Reset delivery promises or fix carrier SLAs.** A 40.6% late rate across all carriers points to promise-date setting rather than one carrier. Validate with carrier SLA terms.

## Limitations

- Modeled operating profit excludes depreciation, interest, tax and complete overhead. Not GAAP.
- Marketing efficiency is blended net sales per allocated spend. No attributed ROAS, CAC or LTV is possible.
- October 2026 returns are immature; return comparisons for that month understate final rates.
- Repeat share depends on window length; compare only matching periods.
- Membership tier is current tier, not tier at time of order.
- No inventory, funnel, or supplier cost data, so causes of conversion, return and margin gaps are not testable here.
